
# 📊 Investment Insights Dashboard 

## 🧭 Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of investor demographics, investment types, savings objectives, and behavioral trends.  
Developed as part of the **Cognifyz Data Analytics Project**, it helps visualize and interpret key investment patterns and preferences.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Understand and summarize key statistics in the dataset.  
- Analyze gender-based differences in investment preferences.  
- Examine investment duration and monitoring frequency.  
- Identify common savings objectives and reasons for investment.  
- Visualize insights through an interactive and professional Power BI dashboard.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard creation  
- **CSV Dataset** – Data storage and preparation  

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- Average investor age: **33 years**  
- **Mutual Funds** are the most preferred investment type.  
- **Internet** is the top source of financial information.  
- **Retirement** is the most common savings objective.  
- Most investors monitor their investments **monthly**.

## 🧩 Dashboard Sections
1. **Data Exploration and Summary** – Overview of dataset and basic statistics.  
2. **Gender-Based Analysis** – Comparison of male vs. female investment behavior.  
3. **Investment Duration and Frequency** – Insights into monitoring patterns.  
4. **Source of Information** – Channels where investors get their financial info.  
5. **Reasons for Investment** – Key motivators behind investment decisions.  
6. **Comprehensive Dashboard** – Final combined view with interactivity and insights.
